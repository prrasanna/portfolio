About Me

Hi, I'm Bala Prasanna M from Dindigul, a passionate Full Stack Web Developer with a strong interest in building modern, responsive, and user-friendly web applications. I have experience working with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB. My interest in web development began after participating in hackathons and being inspired by innovative projects developed by other teams and my college seniors. Since then, I have continuously improved my skills through hands-on practice and real-world projects. I have participated in hackathons such as Zoho CRM Hackathon, HackIndia, and TN Impact Hackathon, where I developed AI-powered applications and gained practical experience in MERN Stack development, REST API development, third-party API integration, JWT authentication, MongoDB, Redis, and Postman. These experiences strengthened my problem-solving, teamwork, and software development skills while helping me understand real-world business requirements. I enjoy learning new technologies, exploring innovative solutions, and continuously improving my technical skills. My goal is to build impactful web applications and grow as a Full Stack Web Developer by contributing to meaningful and innovative software solutions.
